We build USAI Wallet, an AI-powered global trade matching platform that helps exporters find reliable overseas buyers and complete each trade with verified documents.

What we do

K-AI CONET WYOMING LLC is an early-stage technology and software development company. Our platform, USAI Wallet, uses AI to match exporters with suitable overseas buyers based on product, market and trade requirements.

Alongside matching, the platform gives both sides practical tools: trade document management, shipment tracking with ocean carriers, and customs and bill of lading verification, so a buyer can confirm that goods have actually shipped.

How a trade works on our platform

  1. MatchingAn exporter lists its products, and the platform suggests suitable overseas buyers.
  2. Agreement and documentsThe parties agree on terms, and trade documents such as the commercial invoice and packing list are prepared and recorded.
  3. Direct paymentThe buyer pays the seller directly by bank transfer. We do not hold, manage or transmit customer funds.
  4. Shipment verificationShipping and customs records are checked so both parties can see the cargo has been loaded and cleared for export.

Intellectual property

The company owns the platform's software and intellectual property. Patent applications covering the matching and verification technology have been filed in the United States and in Korea.
